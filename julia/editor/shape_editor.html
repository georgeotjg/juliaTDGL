Shape editor

the box starts full of superconductor; what you draw is removed (f=0) or gets its own f · each node is a voxel

Work face

Pick the face you work from, here or by clicking it on the 3D model: its view is outlined and the page scrolls to it. Each click of the pencil or the eraser goes depth cells into the sample from that face, starting from cells inside. from 0 + through = the whole thickness; from > 0 = a cut inside the sample. Figures (circle, rectangle…) use the same from/depth when their z is "from the work face" (top or bottom): a circle 3 cells deep from the top, or a buried disc from 2 to 3. The list of figures says which z planes each one covers, and its z0/z1 can be edited there.

Tool

pencil / eraser: paint cells on any view (width in cells). Figures (circle, rectangle…): drag on the top view, f and z below. gradient: drag a rectangle; f or depth changes along it in n steps. select/move: drag a figure or the pencil layer. move cuts: click a view to move the other cuts there.
keys Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · [ ] lower/raise the z plane · right click on any view = move the cuts there · Del deletes the selected figure · r/R rotates ±5° · arrows move · Enter closes the polygon · Esc cancels

Pencil palette

Each character is a value of f: 0 removes the material, 0 < f < 1 weakens it, f > 1 strengthens it. Click one to paint with it.
The pencil paints the selected character: its f is what the material gets. "." is untouched material; ✕ deletes a character and what it painted.
legacy mode (z extent per character)
