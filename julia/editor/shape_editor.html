Shape editor

the box starts full of superconductor; what you draw gets its own f (0 = normal metal) or becomes vacuum (no material: release 2) · each node is a voxel

Work face

Pick the face you work from, here or by clicking it on the 3D model: its view is outlined and the page scrolls to it. Each click of the pencil or the eraser goes depth cells into the sample from that face, starting from cells inside; picking a face sets depth to the whole sample along its normal (through). from > 0 = a cut inside the sample. Figures (circle, rectangle…) are drawn on the view of the work face: top/bottom on the Top view (extruded along z), front/back on the Front view (along y), left/right on the Side view (along x). Their extent along that axis is the same from/depth ("from the work face") or any range; the list of figures shows it and edits it.

Tool

pencil / eraser: paint cells on any view (width in cells). Figures (circle, rectangle…): drag on any view; the figure goes through the sample along the axis of that view. "keep the figure" writes f (or vacuum) outside it instead of inside: the figure is what stays (a cable). "vacuum" removes the material instead of giving it an f: no superconductor, no normal metal (release 2, with an insulating padding). gradient: drag a rectangle on the top view; f or depth changes along it in n steps. select/move: drag a figure or the pencil layer. move cuts: click a view to move the other cuts there.
slabs along z: n gap planes
keys Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · [ ] lower/raise the z plane · right click on any view = move the cuts there · Del deletes the selected figure · r/R rotates ±5° · arrows move · Shift while dragging a rectangle: a square · polygon: a click adds a vertex, Backspace or Ctrl+Z removes the last, Enter or a double click closes it, Esc cancels

Pencil palette

Each character is a value of f: 0 removes the material, 0 < f < 1 weakens it, f > 1 strengthens it. Click one to paint with it.
The pencil paints the selected character: its f is what the material gets. "." is untouched material; ✕ deletes a character and what it painted.
legacy mode (z extent per character)
