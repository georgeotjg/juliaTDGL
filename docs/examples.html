Examples

One run file per option in tdgl2/examples/, each run by one command. Times are on one core of a laptop. The short ones are demonstrations of an option, not measurements.

OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 julia --project=tdgl2 tdgl2/scripts/run.jl tdgl2/examples/<file>.toml

The notched box

A box of two bands with a wedge cut into its face y1. With the field along z and the current along x, vortices enter through y1 for +J and through y2 for −J. The notch lowers the barrier on one side only, so the two onsets differ.

A sculpted nanowire

A disc of radius 2.75 ξ along the current, with vacuum around it and leads of its own section. The vacuum needs the insulating buffer. J is the current density, so the cable carries J times its meshed section.

Materials per region

bicapa_tunel.toml stacks two slabs of 3 ξ along z: the two-band model at κ = 2 below, and above it a slower material with a quarter of the diffusivity in both bands and a weaker σ band. A barrier of transparency 0.2 joins them. The shape file names the upper region material = "lenta", and the run file defines it in a [materials.lenta] table: inline bands, or a material file of the catalogue (file = "mgb2_dirty.toml").

KWT, boundaries, heat, three bands

  • kwt_k2.toml: KWT in both bands raises the onset by the conversion partition. The stepper turns on the rotating frame and the stabilised stages by itself.
  • open_k2.toml: the faces see the free-space field of the screening currents, which a mirror face suppresses at small κ. The two polarities separate more.
  • heat_k25.toml: a temperature field with the Joule term and the contact layers left out of the source, and interband drag. A rung whose temperature passes the cut closes T and ends the ladder.
  • three_bands.toml: three identical bands with equal repulsive couplings. The phases settle at 0 and ±2π/3, and |Ψ|² = 1 + |γ| per band.
